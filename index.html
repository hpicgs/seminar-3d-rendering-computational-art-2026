Introduction

The seminar focuses on the conception and implementation of small but high-quality computer graphics applications. Each project is expected to unite technical excellence with artistic intention. From a technical perspective, it should make use of contemporary interactive 3D rendering methods. From a conceptual perspective, it should be guided by a distinct artistic idea that gives the application its purpose, coherence, and identity. The artistic idea is not an ornament added afterwards; it is the central motivation of the work. There is no product idea in the conventional sense. What matters instead is the quality and harmony of image, animation, sound, motion, atmosphere, and interaction.

Scope and Context

We at the research group for Computer Graphics Systems, chaired by Prof. Dr. Jürgen Döllner, designed a project seminar at the intersection of advanced computer graphics, computational art, and agentic software development. With six Bachelor's students, we conducted this seminar named "3D Rendering & Computational Art" in the summer semester of 2026 at Hasso Plattner Institute, Digital Engineering Faculty, University of Potsdam. Projects

Unstill Life – Vita Perpetua

Contributor:  Yegi Park

The still life is a traditional art genre that depicts inanimate objects carefully arranged in a controlled space. This project extends the genre into a cosmic dimension. The room slowly darkens into outer space, and the objects drift into weightlessness before fading away entirely. Four objects stand for four forms of existence on Earth: a stone as inanimate matter, a flower as plant, a teddy bear as animal, and a wooden mannequin as human. Their quiet disappearance carries the central idea that, from a cosmic perspective, all things are equally transient. They appear briefly, dissolve, and return in a new form as part of an endless cycle.

T-1003

Contributor:  Jonas Vinmann

This interactive 3D prototype centers on an abstract, non-anthropomorphic creature. It is caught in an endless cycle of composure breaking under the very gaze it tries so desperately to protect itself against. Its deceptively perfect surface cannot entirely conceal how fragile it remains underneath its facade. The moment it notices it is being observed, it bursts open, revealing its true self and scattering its distorted components through space. Only once it believes itself unobserved does it reassemble and resume its attempt to mask its undeniably alien nature. The demo invites viewers to reflect on how readily we project human emotions onto a purely artificial construct, and perhaps to recognize something of themselves in its Sisyphean effort.

Lava Landscape

Contributor:  Shyue-Shyang Alexander Ho

What happens to a still life that never stands still? Unstill Life: Lava Landscape invites you to slow down and explore a volcanic landscape where lava, sun, and ash shift almost imperceptibly. Metaballs give the glowing lava its soft, fluid forms. Touch the lava flow and see how the terrain responds.
